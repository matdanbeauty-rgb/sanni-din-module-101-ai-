🎨 Tajweed Academy

A structured learning centre covering the major areas of Qur'anic recitation, from foundations and Makharij to Sifaat, Noon Sakinah, Meem Sakinah, Madd, Waqf and more.

1

🌱 Foundations of Tajweed

Start a complete beginner-friendly lesson with multiple pages, examples, checkpoints and a mini practice activity.

2

👄 Makharij al-Huruf

The major articulation areas are: Al-Jawf, Al-Halq, Al-Lisan, Ash-Shafatan and Al-Khayshum.


Study where each group of Arabic letters originates and practise distinguishing similar sounds.

3

🗣️ Sifaat al-Huruf

Study Hams, Jahr, Shiddah, Rikhawah, Tawassut, Isti'la, Istifal, Itbaq, Infitah, Idhlaq, Ismat, Safir, Qalqalah, Leen, Inhiraf, Takrir, Tafashshi, Istitalah and Ghunnah.

4

🔵 Noon Sakinah & Tanween

The four major rules are Izhar, Idgham, Iqlab and Ikhfa.


Idgham is further divided into with Ghunnah and without Ghunnah.

5

🟢 Meem Sakinah

Study Izhar Shafawi, Ikhfa Shafawi and Idgham Shafawi.

6

📏 Madd Rules

Study Madd Asli and the major types of Madd Far'i, including Wajib Muttasil, Ja'iz Munfasil, Lazim, Arid li's-Sukun, Lin, Badal, Silah and Iwad.

7

⚖️ Tafkheem & Tarqeeq

Learn the difference between heavy and light pronunciation, including the permanent heavy letters and situations that affect pronunciation.

8

ر Rules of Raa

Study circumstances where Raa is pronounced heavy or light and the special cases requiring attention.

9

ل Rules of Lam

Study Lam Shamsiyyah, Lam Qamariyyah and the rules of Lam in Lafzul-Jalalah.

10

🛑 Waqf & Ibtida

Learn the principles of stopping and beginning, including how stopping at the wrong location can affect meaning.

11

✨ Hamzatul Wasl

Study the connecting Hamzah and how pronunciation changes depending on whether the word begins a recitation or follows another word.

12

☀️ Lam Shamsiyyah & Qamariyyah

Learn when the Lam of ال is pronounced clearly and when it is assimilated into the following Sun letter.

Core Rules Reference

Qalqalah

ق ط ب ج د

A bouncing or echoing quality associated with the Qalqalah letters when they have Sukoon.

Ikhfa

نْ / ـً ـٍ ـٌ

Noon Sakinah or Tanween is concealed with Ghunnah before the Ikhfa letters.

Iqlab

نْ + ب

Noon Sakinah or Tanween changes toward a Meem-like sound before Baa.

Idgham

ي ن م و ل ر

Noon Sakinah or Tanween is assimilated into certain following letters.

Izhar Halqi

ء ه ع ح غ خ

Noon Sakinah or Tanween is pronounced clearly before the six throat letters.

Ghunnah

نّ مّ

A nasal sound with prescribed timing, especially prominent with Noon and Meem carrying Shaddah.
