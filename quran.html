Core Tajweed Rules

Qalqalah — قلقلة

ق ط ب ج د

A slight echo or bouncing sound when one of the Qalqalah letters carries a suitable Sukoon.

Ikhfa — إخفاء

نْ / ـً ـٍ ـٌ

A concealed pronunciation of Noon Sakinah or Tanween before the designated letters.

Iqlab — إقلاب

نْ + ب

Noon Sakinah or Tanween changes toward a Meem sound before the letter Baa.

Idgham with Ghunnah

ي ن م و

Assimilation accompanied by nasal resonance.

Idgham without Ghunnah

ل ر

Assimilation into Laam or Raa without the nasal resonance of Ghunnah.

Izhar — إظهار

ء ه ع ح غ خ

Noon Sakinah or Tanween is pronounced clearly before the six throat letters.

Meem Sakinah

مْ

Learn Izhar Shafawi, Ikhfa Shafawi and Idgham Shafawi.

Ghunnah — غنة

نّ مّ

A nasal sound held for the prescribed duration in applicable cases.

Madd — مد

ا و ي

Learn the different types of Qur'anic elongation and their durations.

Rules of Ra — ر

ر

Learn when Raa is pronounced heavy or light according to the surrounding vowels and rules.

Lam Shamsiyyah

الـ

The Lam is assimilated into the following Sun letter in pronunciation.

Hamzatul Wasl

ٱ

Learn how the connecting Hamzah is treated when beginning or continuing recitation.
