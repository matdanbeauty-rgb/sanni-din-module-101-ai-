Core Tajweed Rules

Qalqalah — قلقلة

ق ط ب ج د

A slight echo or bouncing sound when one of the Qalqalah letters carries Sukoon.

Ikhfa — إخفاء

نْ / ـً ـٍ ـٌ

A concealed pronunciation of Noon Sakinah or Tanween before the designated letters.

Iqlab — إقلاب

نْ + ب

Noon Sakinah or Tanween changes toward a Meem sound before Baa.

Idgham with Ghunnah

ي ن م و

Assimilation accompanied by nasal resonance.

Idgham without Ghunnah

ل ر

Assimilation into Laam or Raa without Ghunnah.

Izhar — إظهار

ء ه ع ح غ خ

Noon Sakinah or Tanween is pronounced clearly before the six throat letters.

Meem Sakinah

مْ

Learn Izhar Shafawi, Ikhfa Shafawi and Idgham Shafawi.

Ghunnah — غنة

نّ مّ

A nasal sound held for the prescribed duration.

Madd — مد

ا و ي

Learn the different types of Qur'anic elongation.

Rules of Ra — ر

ر

Learn when Raa is pronounced heavy or light.

Lam Shamsiyyah

الـ

The Lam is assimilated into the following Sun letter in pronunciation.

Hamzatul Wasl

ٱ

Learn how the connecting Hamzah is treated.
